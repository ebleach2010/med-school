# Clinician review notes for built-out modules

Each module below is an AI draft. These are the points the author flagged as needing a clinician's check, plus any claim that rests on a source whose full text could not be opened from the build environment (PubMed, PMC, and most publisher pages were blocked; citation details were confirmed from search results indexing them).

## f-terminology
- Allodynia, hyperalgesia, paresthesia, and dysesthesia follow IASP wording (loeser2008-iasp-terminology), checked against copies of the IASP list, not the paper.
- Limb apraxia after dominant-hemisphere lesions, tested in the nondominant arm when the dominant arm is weak; dysphasia avoided because it sounds like dysphagia.
- m-term-note keys: "nonfluent (expressive) aphasia" and "apraxia, tested in the left hand". m-term-root-loc ties C7 to triceps weakness and the triceps reflex.

## f-cells
- Uhthoff phenomenon, sodium channel drugs, potassium and weakness, benzodiazepine withdrawal seizures, and "hand symptoms once leg symptoms reach the knee" rest on the curriculum anchor only.
- Ganglionopathy item (difficulty 3) may be hard for a foundation module. CIDP "at least 8 weeks" attributed to vandenbergh2021-cidp. Drug-induced parkinsonism wording against bloem2021-pd.

## f-cranial-nerves-all
- Bell palsy red flags and summary (steroids within 72 hours, reassess at 3 months) against baugh2013-bells.
- Lateral medullary features rest on the curriculum source. Simplifications: CN XI placed in the medulla by convention; unilateral upper motor neuron lesion deviates the tongue toward the weak side; face spared in medial medullary syndrome.
- m-cnall-medial-loc: acute tongue deviation without wasting.

## f-sensory-visual-vestibular
- Macular sparing explanation; optic tract lesions causing a contralateral RAPD; fingertips involved when leg symptoms reach the knees.
- HINTS reliability in untrained hands and extra central red flags (vertical nystagmus, cannot stand unaided) are not from kattah2009-hints.
- Anterior vermis in alcohol-related cerebellar degeneration. RAPD key gives 0.6 for a side without a structure.

## f-autonomic-pathways
- Facial anhidrosis by Horner level; orthostatic hypotension definition from freeman2011-oh.

## x-symptoms-signs
- Effort-dependent versus effort-independent sign grouping is teaching convention, cited to the curriculum.

## x-mental-status
- Cue and recognition rule (retrieval versus encoding deficit); whole-body commands preserved with poor comprehension; transcortical aphasia localization; Gerstmann mention; right hemisphere attends to both hemispaces.
- "Receptive aphasia" earns 0.6, not full credit, for Wernicke aphasia.

## x-cn-exam
- Exam order (acuity before bright light); "an RAPD does not come from cataract"; "urgent imaging before LP" for disc swelling; angle-of-jaw sparing; gag reflex adds little when palate, voice, and swallow are normal.

## x-coordination-gait
- Rebound test technique; cerebellar pathways "cross twice"; Romberg's original description in tabes dorsalis.
- Functional gait signs against daum2014-positive-signs; NPH versus Parkinson disease turning and base width; partial credit in two localization items.

## x-coma
- Naloxone has no specific source. Thiamine "before or with" glucose without delaying glucose. Pontine localization partial credit. Posturing levels taught as classical with caveats. No doses, no GCS cutoff.

## c-tia-secondary
- Recommendations from kleindorfer2021-secondary (workup within 48 hours, anticoagulation for AF, short-term dual antiplatelets, blood pressure below 130/80) were read in summaries, not full text. No DAPT durations, stenosis thresholds, or ABCD2 cutoffs.
- Lacunar item treats a moderate contralateral carotid stenosis as incidental.

## c-ich-sah
- Blood pressure target and reversal agents (andexanet alfa or PCC for factor Xa inhibitors) from guideline summaries; no doses.
- Hyponatremia "avoid fluid restriction" and delayed cerebral ischemia timing not confirmed from a source. Sentinel headache answer (CTA plus LP at 9 days) and cerebellar surgery indications.

## c-cvt-dissection
- Thrombolysis in dissection attributed to powers2019-ais; check against prabhakaran2026-ais.
- ESO 2017 preference for LMWH over DOACs presented beside the 2024 AHA view. RCVS "reversal within about three months" and CVT "death or dependence 10 to 15 percent" from saposnik2024-cvt.

## c-nmosd-mogad
- Claims tied to kumpfel2024-nemos (early escalation to plasma exchange or immunoadsorption, preventive therapy after a first attack, MS drugs that can worsen NMOSD) are mainstream but were not read in the paper.
- No MOG titer cutoffs taught; content/antibodies.ts states 1:100 for fixed assays, which should be checked against banwell2023-mogad. MOGAD supporting features written from knowledge of Banwell 2023, not its text.

## c-headache
- Giant cell arteritis wording ("biopsy within about two weeks of starting glucocorticoids", IV pulse when vision is threatened) rests on a summary of maz2021-acr-gca, not its text.
- Preventive drug classes, "avoid opioids and barbiturates", and the triptan vascular contraindication are stated more broadly than the cited sources. ICHD-3 medication overuse day counts are quoted. SNNOOP10 older-age cutoff deliberately not given.

## c-ms
- The 2024 McDonald changes are described only in general terms; compare with montalban2025-mcdonald itself (not opened here).
- "Some MS treatments can worsen NMOSD" attributed to wingerchuk2015-nmosd; anti-CD20 benefit in primary progressive MS; plasma exchange for steroid-refractory relapse; mimic list in m-ms-course.
- Integration fix: "dissemination in space and oligoclonal bands" now earns 0.6 (bands can substitute for evidence of dissemination in time since 2017) instead of being marked a misconception.

## c-epilepsy-mimics
- Sleep deprivation taught as a trigger to ask about, not as making a seizure provoked (consistent with ILAE usage). daum2014-positive-signs cited only for the principle of positive signs.
- ECG and pregnancy test graded "reasonable" rather than "necessary" in m-epimim-first-tests. 21 to 45 percent two-year recurrence from krumholz2015-first-seizure. Limb-shaking TIA and frontal lobe seizures mentioned briefly.

## c-spinal-emergencies
- Steroid caution when lymphoma is possible: standard teaching, not confirmed in nice2023-ng234. No dexamethasone dose or blood pressure targets.
- From darouiche2006-sea: avoid LP, empiric gram-negative cover, full triad in a minority. Antibiotics placed alongside MRI in a febrile patient with a deficit.
- From lavy2009-ces: CES-I and CES-R and the quoted 43 percent false positive rate. Incomplete cord syndromes cite the curriculum only. ISNCSCI has been revised since 2011.

## c-polyneuropathy-radiculopathy
- m-pnr-upper-trunk places the lesion in the upper trunk because rhomboids and serratus anterior are normal; root avulsion earns partial credit.
- First-line screen marks HbA1c, B12 with methylmalonic acid, and protein electrophoresis with immunofixation as necessary; TSH and nerve studies reasonable.
- Metformin and low B12, the chemotherapy drug list, and mononeuritis multiplex blood tests are standard teaching without a specific source.
- Grader limit: "mononeuritis multiplex, rule out polyarteritis" earns 0.6 because "rule out" reads as negation; changing that globally made diagnosis answers worse, so it was left as is.

## c-encephalitis-viral
- Toxoplasmosis advice (empiric treatment, biopsy if no response) is cited to the abscess review and the IDSA 2008 encephalitis guideline, not an HIV opportunistic infection guideline.
- Repeat HSV PCR at 24 to 48 hours follows solomon2012-uk-enceph; IDSA may differ. Select-tests categories for VZV and enterovirus PCR and HIV testing. Cryptococcal drainage threshold from a perfect2010-idsa-crypto excerpt. No acyclovir doses.

## c-myopathy-mnd
- Breaking bad news (m-myomnd-news) is standard teaching cited only to feldman2022-als and the curriculum.
- Multifocal motor neuropathy statements (IVIG response, steroids may worsen, anti-GM1) and the ALS mimic list rely on feldman2022-als, not confirmed against its text. Dystrophinopathy and rhabdomyolysis content has no specific source. Gold Coast criteria checked against summaries only.
- Several PMIDs came from secondary listings (reference lists, catalogs, PubMed mirrors); each verification note says where.

## c-parkinsonism
- A cogwheel catch alone not meeting the MDS definition of rigidity; MDS supportive features, red flags, and exclusions paraphrased without time windows.
- Tic and myoclonus definitions, non-Huntington causes of chorea, the serotonin syndrome comparison, and Wilson disease screening in young patients rest only on the curriculum source. Alcohol response in essential tremor; NMS risk factors and the mention of dantrolene or bromocriptine (no doses).
- bates2015-hd DOI recorded as 10.1038/nrdp.2015.5; one PDF footer showed nrdp.2015.52. Confirm by opening the link.

## c-dementia
- Anticoagulation advice in the anti-amyloid item follows the 2023 lecanemab appropriate use recommendations; donanemab not covered.
- m-dem-workup marks B12, TSH, MRI, and depression and sleep screening as necessary. Antipsychotic escalation wording "senior or specialist input". bvFTD onset often before 65 has no specific citation. Vascular cognitive impairment rests on the curriculum source.

## c-toxic-nutritional
- Sodium correction limits (10 mmol/L in the first 24 hours, then 8 per 24 hours) attributed to spasovski2014-hyponatraemia, confirmed only from secondary summaries.
- "Give glucose now, thiamine with or right after" anchored to galvin2010-wernicke; Sechi wording not checked. Hypoglycemia, hepatic encephalopathy, uremia, lithium, and carbon monoxide cite the curriculum only. kumar2006-copper DOI from a citing reference.
- Integration fix: a bare "Wernicke" now earns 0.7 while "Wernicke aphasia" stays wrong.

## c-vertigo-vision
- From GRACE-3 and the CRAO statement, read in abstracts and summaries: finger-rub hearing check, gait severity, steroids for vestibular neuritis, observational evidence for thrombolysis within 4.5 hours in CRAO.
- Meniere disease, vestibular migraine, and nonarteritic ischemic optic neuropathy features are general teaching. m-vv-bppv-actions labels routine vestibular suppressants "harmful", stronger than the guideline's "recommend against".

## c-paraneoplastic
- m-pns-definite grades limbic encephalitis with small cell lung cancer and no antibody as "probable"; the label rests on the author's knowledge of PNS-Care scoring (no point values taught). Intermediate-risk phenotype examples hedged but not confirmed against graus2021-pns.
- EFNS screening intervals (repeat at 3 to 6 months, then every 6 months up to 4 years; 2 years for LEMS) predate the 2021 criteria.
- Integration fix: the grader no longer treats neuropathy and neuronopathy (or myopathy and myelopathy, dysphagia and dysphasia) as typos of each other.

## c-rpd
- Prion infection control (special handling for brain, spinal cord, and eye instruments) rests on geschwind2016-rpd and hermann2021-scjd; WHO or CDC guidance would be better (see proposed sources).
- Tension between "steroids can make a lymphoma biopsy nondiagnostic" and "do not delay immunotherapy"; check the framing. Immunotherapy item is LGI1-like and assumes Graus criteria are met. RT-QuIC called highly specific without figures; 14-3-3 and tau less specific; variant and genetic forms in one line each.

## c-autonomic
- Parkinson disease and DLB grouped as central causes with a note that postganglionic sympathetic nerves also degenerate (spared in MSA). Heart rate response ratio taught in principle only.
- Sweat item reads abnormal thermoregulatory sweat test with normal QSART as preganglionic. Features against MSA and "ganglionic AChR" wording against the papers. Fludrocortisone, midodrine, droxidopa, and a bedtime short-acting antihypertensive for supine hypertension named without doses. Initial orthostatic hypotension thresholds from freeman2011-oh.

## c-sleep
- MSLT at a principles level without cutoffs. m-sleep-narco-tests marks a stimulant started before testing as "harmful".
- Sleep-related hypermotor epilepsy named from the ILAE hyperkinetic-onset category; no dedicated consensus paper cited. Most older adults with idiopathic RBD said to later develop a synucleinopathy, without rates.
- winkelman2025-aasm-rls iron and augmentation recommendations and aasm2023-icsd3tr claims confirmed only from secondary summaries.

## c-neurocritical
- Only numbers: trauma ICP threshold 22 mm Hg and CPP 60 to 70 mm Hg from carney2017-btf-tbi, checked against summaries and labeled trauma-specific. No temperature, CO2, or blood pressure thresholds for brain death testing.
- From summaries of greer2023-bd-dnc, not full text: EEG not accepted as an ancillary test; ancillary tests only when the clinical evaluation cannot be completed; spinal reflexes can persist; donation request separate from telling the family.
- Head of bed elevation, loosening neck collars, treating fever and seizures cite general teaching. m-neurocrit-herniation-order sequence (first-line measures before repeat CT and neurosurgical review, which run together).

## c-fnd
- "DSM-5-TR keeps the no-stressor rule" rests on espay2018-fnd, which describes DSM-5. Hip abductor sign mechanics from the Sonoo abstract. Functional facial spasm versus hemifacial spasm rests on one small study.
- m-fnd-tests labels a deceptive saline "test" injection harmful. Physiotherapy wording on walking aids.

## c-neuro-oncology
- Steroids before biopsy when lymphoma is possible rests on schaff2018-pcnsl, attributed in search results; full text not read. Steroid and seizure prophylaxis wording in the brain metastasis guideline not seen directly.
- WHO molecular criteria for glioblastoma (TERT, EGFR, chromosome 7 gain and 10 loss) from the WHO summary paper. Ring-tests item marks antiseizure medication after one tumor-related seizure as necessary. VTE anticoagulation wording with a brain tumor.

## c-pediatric
- Milestone red flag ages (walking by about 18 months, words by about 16 months, two-word phrases by about 24 months, hand preference before 12 months) are common teaching, not confirmed in lipkin2020-developmental-surveillance.
- Early SMA treatment and newborn screening, and vigabatrin first-line for infantile spasms in TSC, rest on summaries (mercuri2018-sma-part1 covers diagnosis; treatment is in part 2). Infantile spasms described as "usually in the first year" with hypsarrhythmia as classic.
- m-peds-febrile-lp CT-before-LP and "do not delay antibiotics" options cite the adult meningitis guideline beside the AAP guideline.

## c-neuropsychiatry
- Antipsychotic caution in catatonia and the NMS and malignant catatonia overlap: rogers2023-catatonia wording not confirmed. Red flag list from pollak2020-autoimmune-psychosis checked against summaries only.
- Age thresholds ("after about 40 to 50") are teaching convention, not sourced. Antipsychotic intolerance in NMDA receptor encephalitis. Medication and substance causes rest on general sources.
- m-neuropsych-nmdar is close to placement item p-immuno-nmdar-1 (differs by setting and asks for the next step).
