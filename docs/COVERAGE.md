## Known gaps in the antibody reference

- **Neurexin-3 alpha**: Encephalitis described in few patients; emerging, limited validation; no verified source in registry yet.
- **Dopamine D2 receptor**: Reported in pediatric basal ganglia encephalitis and Sydenham chorea; mainly research assays with inconsistent replication; no verified source in registry yet.
- **Homer-3**: Cerebellar ataxia in case reports and small series; emerging, limited validation.
- **ITPR1**: Cerebellar ataxia and neuropathy in small series; emerging, limited validation.
- **AP3B2**: Cerebellar ataxia and sensory neuronopathy in small series; emerging.
- **Septins (septin-5, septin-7)**: Cerebellar ataxia and encephalopathy in small series; emerging.
- **PDE10A**: Paraneoplastic chorea in small series; emerging.
- **TRIM46, TRIM9, TRIM67**: Paraneoplastic cerebellar and encephalitic syndromes in small series; emerging.
- **ANNA-3 (DACH1)**: Paraneoplastic syndromes with lung cancer; limited data; no verified source in registry yet.
- **Zic4**: Associated with small cell lung cancer and cerebellar syndromes, often alongside other antibodies; no registry source reviewed for this draft.
- **KCTD16**: Co-occurs with GABA-B receptor antibodies and associated with small cell lung cancer; no verified source in registry yet.
- **Other recently described neural antibodies (for example neurochondrin, AK5, Sez6l2, GluK2, Kv1.4)**: Emerging, limited validation; no verified source in registry yet.
- **N-type voltage-gated calcium channel**: Low clinical specificity; registry sources do not support a full entry.
- **Ganglionic acetylcholine receptor (alpha3)**: Autoimmune autonomic ganglionopathy; well described in the literature but no verified source in registry yet (proposed in docs/proposed-sources.md).
- **FGFR3**: Sensory neuronopathy; emerging, limited validation.
- **TS-HDS**: Proposed marker for small fiber neuropathy; limited validation and specificity concerns.
- **Additional ganglioside antibodies (GD1a, GD1b, GT1a, GalNAc-GD1a, GM2, sulfatide)**: Associations with Guillain-Barré variants and chronic neuropathies exist, but registry sources do not support separate entries; GD1a and GT1a are mentioned only within other entries.
- **IgM anti-GM1 in multifocal motor neuropathy (full entry)**: No multifocal motor neuropathy guideline in registry yet (proposed in docs/proposed-sources.md).
- **Recoverin and other retinal antibodies**: Outside current scope; no registry source; known assay specificity concerns.
- **ANCA**: Relevant to vasculitic neuropathy and CNS vasculitis evaluation; no registry source covering neurological use.
- **Antiphospholipid antibodies**: Relevant to stroke in young patients and other neurological syndromes; no registry source.
- **ANA and ENA**: Systemic autoimmune screening is catalogued as an investigation (ana-ena), but no registry source supports a neurological antibody entry.
- **Celiac serology (tissue transglutaminase, deamidated gliadin, gliadin)**: Gluten ataxia and neuropathy associations are debated; no registry source.
- **Myositis-specific and myositis-associated antibodies (for example HMGCR, SRP, Jo-1)**: Outside current scope; no registry source.
- **NMDAR NR2 peptide antibodies (lupus)**: Different antigen and assay from GluN1 cell-based testing; uncertain significance; no registry source.
- **Ribosomal P antibodies**: Neuropsychiatric lupus association uncertain; no registry source.
